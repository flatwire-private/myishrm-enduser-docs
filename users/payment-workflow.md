# Admission & Payment Workflow

How applications are processed from submission to enrollment.

---

## Overview

This guide explains the complete enrollment process, from when you submit your application to when you become a student.

---

## The Complete Workflow

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    ENROLLMENT WORKFLOW                          │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                 │
│  Step 1: APPLICANT submits application online                   │
│                         ↓                                       │
│  Step 2: ADMISSION reviews and approves → invoice generated     │
│                         ↓                                       │
│  Step 3: APPLICANT pays and uploads payment proof               │
│                         ↓                                       │
│  Step 4: ACCOUNTING verifies the payment                        │
│                         ↓                                       │
│  Step 5: REGISTRAR converts applicant to student                │
│                                                                 │
│                    Welcome, Student!                            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Step 1: Application Submission

**Who:** Applicant

**What happens:**
1. Create an account at the application portal
2. Fill out the application form completely
3. Upload all required documents:
   - Valid ID
   - Birth Certificate
   - Transcript of Records / Form 137
   - 2x2 ID Photo
4. Submit the application

**Result:** Application status becomes "Pending"

**See:** [Applicant Guide](applicant.md)

---

## Step 2: Admission Approval

**Who:** Admission Officer

**What happens:**
1. Admission reviews your application
2. Verifies all documents are complete and clear
3. May schedule an interview (if required)
4. Approves the application
5. System generates an invoice automatically

**Result:**
- Application status becomes "Approved"
- Invoice is created with your admission fees
- You receive notification with payment instructions

**See:** [Admission Officer Guide](admission-officer.md)

---

## Step 3: Payment Upload

**Who:** Applicant

**What happens:**
1. View your invoice in the portal
2. Pay the fees through:
   - Bank deposit/transfer
   - Cash payment at school
   - Other approved methods
3. Upload your payment proof:
   - Take a photo or scan of your receipt
   - Enter payment details (amount, date, reference number)
   - Upload the file
4. Submit for verification

**Result:** Payment status becomes "Pending Verification"

**Tips:**
- Make sure receipt is clear and readable
- Include the reference/transaction number
- Upload within the due date

**See:** [Applicant Guide - Payment Section](applicant.md#step-6-pay-admission-fees)

---

## Step 4: Payment Verification

**Who:** Accounting Staff

**What happens:**
1. Accounting reviews your payment proof
2. Verifies against bank records:
   - Amount matches invoice?
   - Reference number is valid?
   - Payment date is correct?
3. Approves or rejects the payment

**If Approved:**
- Invoice status becomes "Paid"
- You're now ready for student conversion

**If Rejected:**
- You'll see the rejection reason
- Upload a new payment proof with corrections

**See:** [Accounting Staff Guide](accounting-staff.md)

---

## Step 5: Student Conversion

**Who:** Registrar

**What happens:**
1. Registrar sees you're ready for conversion
2. Reviews your application one final time
3. Converts you to a student
4. System creates your student account

**Result:**
- You receive your Student ID (e.g., 2025-00001)
- You receive login credentials
- You can now access the Student Portal!

**See:** [Registrar Guide](registrar.md)

## Tracking Your Progress

You can track your application status at any time:

| Status | Stage | What to Do |
|--------|-------|------------|
| **Pending** | Step 1 | Wait for review |
| **Under Review** | Step 2 | Wait for decision |
| **For Interview** | Step 2 | Attend scheduled interview |
| **Approved** | Step 2 Complete | Pay and upload proof |
| **Payment Pending** | Step 3 | Wait for verification |
| **Payment Verified** | Step 4 Complete | Wait for conversion |
| **Enrolled** | Step 5 Complete | Login as student! |

## Timeline

| Step | Typical Duration |

| Student Conversion | 1 business day |

## Common Questions

**Q: Why can't Admission convert me directly to a student?**
A: The workflow ensures proper payment verification before enrollment. This protects both you and the school.

**Q: What if I paid but my status doesn't change?**
A: Make sure you uploaded your payment proof. Accounting needs to verify it before your status updates.

**Q: Can I skip any steps?**
A: No, all steps must be completed in order. Each role has a specific responsibility in the process.

**Q: How do I know when I'm enrolled?**
A: You'll receive a notification with your Student ID and login credentials. Your status will show "Enrolled".

**Q: What if my payment is rejected?**
A: Read the rejection reason, correct the issue, and upload a new payment proof.
