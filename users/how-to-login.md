# How to Login

This guide shows you how to access the MyISHRM portal.

---

## Step-by-Step Login

### Step 1: Open the Portal

Go to: **[https://myishrm.flatwire.io](https://myishrm.flatwire.io)**

### Step 2: Enter Your Credentials

1. **Email** - Enter your school email address
2. **Password** - Enter your password

### Step 3: Click "Login"

After successful login, you will be redirected to your dashboard based on your role.

---

## First-Time Login

### For New Students
- Your login credentials are provided by the Registrar after admission
- Email: Usually your school email (e.g., `yourname@ishrm.edu.ph`)
- Password: Initial password provided by Registrar

### For New Staff/Faculty
- Contact the System Administrator for your account
- You will receive your email and initial password

### For Applicants
- Create an account during the application process
- Use the email you provided during application

---

## Forgot Your Password?

1. Click **"Forgot Password"** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your email for a password reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password

**Note:** If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

---

## Login Problems?

### "Invalid email or password"
- Double-check your email spelling
- Make sure CAPS LOCK is off
- Try resetting your password

### "Account is locked"
- Contact the System Administrator
- Your account may have been locked after multiple failed attempts

### "Session expired"
- The system automatically logs you out after inactivity
- Simply login again

---

## After Login

Based on your role, you will see your personalized dashboard:

| Role | Dashboard Shows |
|------|-----------------|
| Student | Grades, Schedule, Payments |
| Faculty | Classes, Grading Tasks |
| Admission | Pending Applications |
| Registrar | Enrollments, Documents |
| Accounting | Payment Verifications |
