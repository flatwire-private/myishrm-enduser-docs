# Internal Messaging

Send and receive messages within the MyISHRM portal.

---

## Overview

The messaging feature allows you to:
- Send messages to other portal users
- Receive messages from faculty, staff, or students
- Get notifications for new messages
- View message history

---

## How to Use Messaging

### Access Messages

1. Click **"Messages"** in the sidebar menu
2. Or click the message icon in the top navigation bar

---

### View Your Messages

**Inbox:**
- See all received messages
- Unread messages are highlighted
- Most recent messages appear first

**Each Message Shows:**
- Sender name
- Subject/preview
- Date and time
- Read/unread status

---

### Send a New Message

**Steps:**
1. Click **"New Message"** or **"Compose"**
2. Search for recipient by name
3. Select the recipient from search results
4. Type your message
5. Click **"Send"**

**Tips:**
- Be clear and concise
- Include relevant details (subject name, date, etc.)
- Check spelling before sending

---

### Reply to a Message

1. Open the message you want to reply to
2. Click **"Reply"**
3. Type your response
4. Click **"Send"**

---

### Message Notifications

**You'll be notified when:**
- You receive a new message
- Someone replies to your message

**Notification appears:**
- As a red badge on the message icon
- In your notification panel

---

## Who Can You Message?

**As a Student:**
- Faculty members
- Registrar staff
- Other school staff

**As Faculty:**
- Students in your classes
- Other faculty members
- School staff

**As Staff:**
- Other staff members
- Faculty members
- Students (as needed)

---

## Message Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use professional language
- Be respectful and courteous
- Keep messages relevant to school matters
- Respond to messages promptly

**Don't:**
- Send spam or irrelevant messages
- Use inappropriate language
- Share sensitive personal information
- Harass or bully others

---

## Common Questions

**Q: Are messages private?**
A: Messages are between sender and recipient only. Administrators may access messages if needed for official purposes.

**Q: Can I delete messages?**
A: You can delete messages from your inbox, but the recipient still has their copy.

**Q: Is there a character limit?**
A: Messages can be up to 10,000 characters.

**Q: Can I attach files?**
A: File attachments are not currently supported. Share files through appropriate channels.
