# Clearances

Clearances confirm that a student has fulfilled all obligations to each department before records are processed or a semester is officially closed.

---

## Overview

At the end of each semester, students must obtain clearance from multiple departments. A student is only considered fully cleared when all departments mark them as **Cleared**.

**Clearance Types:**

| Type | Issued By |
|---|---|
| **Faculty** | Faculty members |
| **Accounting** | Accounting Staff |
| **Library** | Library |
| **Registrar** | Registrar |
| **Dean** | Dean's Office |
| **Student Affairs** | Student Affairs |
| **Property** | Property/Assets Office |

**Clearance Statuses:**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Not yet reviewed |
| **Cleared** | All obligations met |
| **Not Cleared** | Outstanding obligation — must be resolved |
| **Conditional** | Partially met — specific condition must be fulfilled |

---

## For Students

### Check Your Clearance Status

1. Click **"Clearances"** in the sidebar menu
2. View the clearance status for each department
3. For any **Not Cleared** or **Conditional** items, contact the relevant department directly

> **Note:** You cannot process your own clearance. Only the responsible department can update your status.

---

## For Accounting Staff

### Manage Financial Clearances

1. Click **"Clearances"** in the sidebar menu
2. View students with pending accounting clearance
3. Check each student's outstanding balance in their account
4. Update the clearance status:
   - **Cleared** — balance is fully settled
   - **Not Cleared** — unpaid balance remains
   - **Conditional** — payment arrangement in place
5. Add a note if applicable
6. Click **"Save"**

---

## For Dean's Office

### Manage College Clearances

1. Click **"Clearances"** in the sidebar menu
2. View students under your college with pending dean clearance
3. Review their academic standing (grades, incomplete subjects, etc.)
4. Update the clearance status:
   - **Cleared** — no outstanding academic obligations
   - **Not Cleared** — incomplete grades, pending requirements, etc.
   - **Conditional** — specific condition attached (add a note)
5. Click **"Save"**

---

## For Registrar — Semester Closure

The Semester Closure process in the Registrar portal consolidates all clearance statuses across departments.

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Semester Closure"** in the menu
2. Select the semester to close
3. Review the clearance summary dashboard:
   - Students fully cleared (all types)
   - Students with one or more pending/not-cleared items
4. Coordinate with Accounting, Dean, and other departments to resolve outstanding cases
5. Once all students are resolved, confirm the semester closure

See [Registrar Guide — Semester Closure](../registrar.md#semester-closure) for the full workflow.

## Common Questions

**Q: What happens if I am Not Cleared?**
A: Contact the department that issued the hold. Resolve the issue (pay a balance, return equipment, complete a requirement), and ask them to update your status.

**Q: Can I graduate with a Conditional clearance?**
A: That depends on the school's policy. Contact the Registrar for guidance.

**Q: Who can see my clearances?**
A: You, the relevant department staff, and the Registrar.
