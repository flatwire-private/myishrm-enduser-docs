# Assignments

Create, submit, and grade assignments within the MyISHRM portal.

---

## Overview

The Assignments feature allows faculty to post work for their classes and students to submit that work online. Both file uploads and text submissions are supported.

---

## For Faculty

### Create an Assignment

1. Click **"Assignments"** in the sidebar menu
2. Click **"Create Assignment"**
3. Fill in the details:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Name of the assignment |
| **Description** | Instructions or details for students |
| **Class** | Select from your assigned sections |
| **Type** | **Assignment** — students submit work; **Instruction** — announcement only |
| **Deadline** | Date and time submissions close |
| **Grace Period** | Optional extra hours accepted after deadline |

4. Attach files if needed (e.g., instruction sheets, templates — up to 5 files)
5. Click **"Save Draft"** to keep it private, or **"Publish"** to make it visible to students immediately

---

### Edit or Close an Assignment

- To edit: Click the assignment → **"Edit"** — only available while in draft or after publishing before the deadline
- To close early: Click the assignment → **"Close"** — no further submissions will be accepted

---

### View Submissions

1. Click on a published assignment
2. Click **"View Submissions"**
3. See the list of students with their submission status:
   - **Submitted** — files uploaded
   - **Not Submitted** — no submission yet
   - **Late** — submitted after the deadline (within grace period)
4. Click on a student's submission to review it

**Download All Submissions:**
- Click **"Download All"** to receive a single ZIP file containing every student's submitted files.

---

### Grade a Submission

1. Open the submission from the submissions list
2. Review the student's uploaded files
3. Enter a **grade** (numeric value per your grading scale)
4. Add optional **feedback** comments
5. Click **"Save Grade"**

Students will see their grade and feedback in their portal.

---

## For Students

### View Your Assignments

1. Click **"Assignments"** in the sidebar menu
2. See all assignments for your enrolled classes
3. Assignments are sorted by deadline — upcoming first
4. Filter by class or status (Pending, Submitted, Graded)

---

### Submit an Assignment

1. Click on an assignment to open it
2. Read the instructions and review any attached files from your faculty
3. Click **"Submit"**
4. Upload your file(s) — up to 5 files per submission
5. Add an optional note to your faculty
6. Click **"Submit Assignment"**

> **Before the deadline:** You can edit your submission. Click the assignment → **"Edit Submission"** → upload new files → **"Resubmit"**.

> **After the deadline:** Submissions are locked unless a grace period is set.

---

### View Grades and Feedback

1. Click on a graded assignment
2. See your grade and any feedback left by your faculty
3. If you believe there is an error, contact your faculty directly

---

## Assignment Status Reference

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Created but not yet published to students |
| **Open** | Published; students can submit |
| **Closed** | Deadline passed or manually closed; no new submissions |

| Submission Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Not Submitted** | Student has not submitted anything |
| **Submitted** | Files uploaded on time |
| **Late** | Submitted after deadline within grace period |
| **Graded** | Faculty has entered a grade |

---

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I submit after the deadline?**
A: Only if your faculty set a grace period. Check the assignment for the grace period duration.

**Q: What file types can I upload?**
A: Common document and image formats. Check the assignment for any specific requirements.

**Q: Can I resubmit after grading?**
A: No. Once graded, submissions are locked.

**Q: Can faculty see my submission before the deadline?**
A: Yes. Faculty can view submissions at any time after you submit.
