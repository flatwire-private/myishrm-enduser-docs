# Grading System

Understanding grades in MyISHRM.

---

## Overview

MyISHRM uses the Philippine grading scale with three grading periods per semester:
1. **Prelims** - First quarter
2. **Midterm** - Second quarter
3. **Finals** - Third quarter

---

## Grade Scale

| Grade | Percentage | Description |
|-------|------------|-------------|
| 1.0 | 97-100 | Excellent |
| 1.25 | 94-96 | Very Good |
| 1.5 | 91-93 | Very Good |
| 1.75 | 88-90 | Very Good |
| 2.0 | 85-87 | Good |
| 2.25 | 82-84 | Good |
| 2.5 | 79-81 | Good |
| 2.75 | 76-78 | Satisfactory |
| 3.0 | 75 | Satisfactory (Passing) |
| 5.0 | Below 75 | Failed |

---

## Special Grades

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| INC | Incomplete - Missing requirements |
| DRP | Dropped - Officially withdrew from subject |
| W | Withdrawn |
| NG | No Grade - Not yet submitted |

## For Students

### Viewing Your Grades

1. Login to your student portal
2. Click **"Grades"** in the menu
3. Select the semester
4. View grades for each subject

**You'll see:**
- Prelims Grade
- Midterm Grade
- Finals Grade
- Final Computed Grade

### When Grades Are Released

- **Prelims:** After prelim exam period
- **Midterm:** After midterm exam period
- **Finals:** After final exam period

**Note:** Grades appear after faculty submits them and they are verified.

### Grade Concerns

If you believe there's an error in your grade:
1. Contact your faculty first
2. If unresolved, file a formal grade appeal with the Registrar
3. Provide supporting documents

---

## For Faculty

### Submitting Grades

1. Login to your faculty portal
2. Click **"Grades"** or **"My Classes"**
3. Select a class
4. Choose the grading period (Prelims/Midterm/Finals)
5. Enter grades for each student
6. Click **"Save"** to save progress
7. Click **"Submit"** when complete

### Grade Entry Tips

- Double-check all grades before submitting
- Use correct grade values (1.0 to 5.0)
- Mark dropped students as "DRP"
- Mark incomplete students as "INC"

### Grading Deadlines

- Check your dashboard for deadline dates
- Yellow warning = Deadline approaching
- Red warning = Deadline today/overdue
- Submit before deadline to avoid issues

---

## Final Grade Computation

The final grade is typically computed as:

```
Final Grade = (Prelims × 30%) + (Midterm × 30%) + (Finals × 40%)
```

**Note:** Computation may vary by subject. Check with your faculty.

---

## Passing Grades

- **3.0** is the minimum passing grade
- Grades of **5.0** mean the subject must be retaken
- **INC** grades must be completed within the specified period

---

## Common Questions

**Q: When will I see my grades?**
A: Grades appear after faculty submits them, usually within 1-2 weeks after each exam period.

**Q: What happens if I get an INC?**
A: You must complete the missing requirements within the specified period (usually one semester). Otherwise, it may become a 5.0.

**Q: Can I see my grade breakdown?**
A: The portal shows the final grade per period. For detailed breakdown, contact your faculty.

**Q: How is GPA calculated?**
A: GPA/GWA is calculated using weighted average of all final grades.
