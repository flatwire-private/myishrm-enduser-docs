# Applicant Guide

**Role:** Applicant
**Primary Responsibility:** Apply for admission to ISHRM

---

## Overview

As an applicant, you will:
- Submit your application online
- Upload required documents
- Pay admission fees
- Upload payment proof
- Track your application status
- Get converted to a student after approval

---

## Application Process

### Step 1: Create Account

1. Go to [https://myishrm.flatwire.io/apply](https://myishrm.flatwire.io/register)
2. Click **"Apply Now"**
3. Create your account:
   - Email address
   - Password
4. Verify your email (check inbox)

---

### Step 2: Fill Application Form

Complete all sections:

**Personal Information:**
- Full name
- Birth date
- Gender
- Civil status
- Nationality
- Contact number

**Address:**
- Complete home address
- City, Province, Zip code

**Educational Background:**
- Last school attended
- Year graduated
- Academic records

**Guardian Information:**
- Guardian name
- Relationship
- Contact number
- Occupation

**Emergency Contact:**
- Contact person name
- Contact number
- Relationship

**Program Selection:**
- Choose your desired program
- Select application type (New/Transferee/Returnee)

---

### Step 3: Upload Documents

**Required Documents:**
- Valid ID (Government-issued)
- Birth Certificate
- Transcript of Records / Form 137
- 2x2 ID Photo

**How to Upload:**
1. Go to **"Documents"** section
2. Click **"Upload"** for each document
3. Select file from your device
4. Wait for upload to complete

**File Requirements:**
- Format: JPEG, PNG, or PDF
- Max size: 5MB per file
- Clear and readable

---

### Step 4: Submit Application

1. Review all information
2. Make sure all documents are uploaded
3. Click **"Submit Application"**
4. Note your **Application Number** (e.g., APP-2026-00001)

---

### Step 5: Wait for Review

After submission:
1. Admission Officer reviews your application
2. You may be contacted for interview
3. Check your application status regularly

**Application Status:**
| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Awaiting review |
| Under Review | Being evaluated |
| For Interview | Interview scheduled |
| Approved | Accepted! Invoice generated |
| Rejected | Not accepted |

---

### Step 6: Pay Admission Fees

**When Approved:**
1. Login to your applicant portal
2. Go to **"Payment"** section
3. View your invoice with:
   - Invoice number
   - Fee breakdown
   - Total amount
   - Due date

**Payment Methods:**
- Bank deposit/transfer
- Cash payment at school
- Other approved methods

---

### Step 7: Upload Payment Proof

**After Paying:**
1. Go to **"Payment"** section
2. Click **"Upload Payment Proof"**
3. Fill in:
   - Amount paid
   - Payment method
   - Payment date
   - Reference number
4. Upload receipt image
5. Click **"Submit"**

---

### Step 8: Wait for Verification

- Accounting staff will verify your payment
- This usually takes 1-2 business days
- Check status in your portal

**Payment Status:**
| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Awaiting verification |
| Verified | Payment confirmed! |
| Rejected | Problem with payment (see reason) |

---

### Step 9: Become a Student

**After Payment Verified:**
1. Registrar converts you to a student
2. You receive your **Student ID** (e.g., 2026-00001)
3. You receive your **login credentials**
4. Login as a student!

---

## Tracking Your Application

**Check Status Anytime:**
1. Login to applicant portal
2. See current status on dashboard
3. View timeline of status changes

**Status Timeline Example:**
```
Jan 5  - Application Submitted
Jan 6  - Under Review
Jan 7  - Approved (Invoice Generated)
Jan 8  - Payment Uploaded
Jan 9  - Payment Verified
Jan 10 - Enrolled as Student!
```

---

## Common Questions

**Q: How long does the review take?**
A: Usually 1-3 business days after complete submission.

**Q: What if my document is rejected?**
A: Re-upload a clearer copy. Check email for specific issues.

**Q: Can I edit my application after submitting?**
A: Contact the Admission Office for corrections.

**Q: What's the payment deadline?**
A: Check your invoice for the due date (usually 7 days).

**Q: What if my payment is rejected?**
A: Read the rejection reason and upload a new proof.
