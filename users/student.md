# Student Guide

**Role:** Student
**Primary Responsibility:** Manage your academic journey

---

## Overview

As a student, you can:
- View your class schedule
- Check your grades
- View and pay tuition fees
- Request official documents
- Send messages to faculty and staff

---

## Login

1. Go to [https://myishrm.flatwire.io](https://myishrm.flatwire.io)
2. Enter your student email and password
3. You'll see your Student Dashboard

---

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard shows:
- **Current Semester** - Active academic period
- **Enrolled Subjects** - Your current classes
- **Grades Summary** - Your academic performance
- **Balance** - Outstanding tuition fees

---

## Key Features

### View Your Grades

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Grades"** in the menu
2. Select the semester to view
3. See your grades for each subject:
   - Prelims Grade
   - Midterm Grade
   - Finals Grade
   - Final Grade (computed)

**Grade Scale:**
| Grade | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 1.0 | Excellent |
| 1.25 - 1.75 | Very Good |
| 2.0 - 2.5 | Good |
| 2.75 - 3.0 | Satisfactory |
| 4.0 | Passed (Conditional) |
| 5.0 | Failed |
| INC | Incomplete |
| DRP | Dropped |

---

### View Your Schedule

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Schedule"** in the menu
2. View your weekly class schedule
3. See details for each class:
   - Subject name
   - Day and time
   - Room/Venue
   - Faculty name

---

### Check Payments

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Payments"** in the menu
2. View your payment history
3. See current balance and due dates

**Payment Status:**
- **Paid** - Payment received and verified
- **Pending** - Payment uploaded, awaiting verification
- **Unpaid** - No payment received yet

---

### Request Documents

**Available Documents:**
- Transcript of Records (TOR)
- Certificate of Enrollment (COE)
- Certificate of Grades (COG)
- Good Moral Certificate
- Other academic documents

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Documents"** in the menu
2. Click **"Request Document"**
3. Select document type
4. Fill in required details
5. Submit request
6. Pay the document fee (if applicable)
7. Wait for processing notification
8. Pick up at Registrar's Office

---

### Send Messages

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Messages"** in the menu
2. Click **"New Message"**
3. Search for recipient (faculty or staff)
4. Type your message
5. Click **"Send"**

---

## Common Questions

**Q: When are grades released?**
A: Grades are released after each grading period (Prelims, Midterm, Finals) once faculty submits them.

**Q: How do I enroll for next semester?**
A: Enrollment opens during the enrollment period. You'll see an "Enroll" option in your dashboard.

**Q: What if my grade is wrong?**
A: Contact your faculty first. If unresolved, file a grade appeal with the Registrar.

**Q: How do I pay my tuition?**
A: Pay at the Cashier's Office or through approved payment channels. Upload your receipt in the Payments section.
