# Accounting Staff Guide

**Role:** Accounting Staff
**Primary Responsibility:** Verify payments and manage student financial accounts

---

## Overview

As Accounting Staff (Accounting Officer / Manager), you can:
- Verify payment proofs uploaded by applicants (admission payments)
- Manage student tuition payments and invoices
- View admission invoices
- View student payment history
- Generate statements of account and receipts
- Manage tuition fee configurations
- Manage student financial clearances
- **Run financial reports** (Account Receivables · Payment Summary · Daily Collection · Payment Summary Details) — see [Reports](features/reports.md)
- **Approve or reject void requests** submitted by cashiers
- Record payments yourself (via the same Payment of Fees screen cashiers use)

If your school has a dedicated cashier at the window, that person uses the narrower **Cashier portal** — see the [Cashier Guide](cashier.md). You retain full visibility into everything they do.

---

## Login

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard shows:

## Key Tasks

### Verify Admission Payment Proofs

**When applicants upload payment proof, you verify it.**

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Payment Proofs"** in the menu
2. See list of pending payment proofs
3. Click **"View Receipt"** to see the uploaded proof
4. Verify against bank records:
   - Amount matches?
   - Reference number valid?
   - Payment date correct?

**To Approve:**
1. Click **"Verify"**
2. Add optional notes (e.g., "Verified via bank confirmation")
3. Click **"Confirm Verification"**

**To Reject:**
1. Click **"Reject"**
2. Enter rejection reason (required, min 10 characters)
3. Click **"Confirm Reject"**

**Result of Verification:**
- Invoice status changes to "Paid"
- Applicant can now be converted to student by Registrar

### Search Student Accounts

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Student Accounts"** in the menu
2. Search by:
   - Student name
   - Student ID
   - Email
3. Click on a student to view their account

---

### View Payment History

**For a specific student:**
1. Search and select the student
2. Click **"Payment History"**
3. View all payments:
   - Payment date
   - Amount
   - Payment method
   - Reference number
   - Verified by

---

### Generate Statement of Account

**Steps:**
1. Search and select a student
2. Click **"Statement of Account"**
3. Select date range
4. View/print the statement showing:
   - All charges
   - All payments
   - Current balance

---

### Manage Tuition Fees

**View Fee Configuration:**
1. Click **"Tuition Fees"** in the menu
2. See fee structures by:
   - Program
   - Year level
   - Fee type (tuition, misc, lab fees)

**Edit Fees:**
1. Click on a fee item
2. Modify the amount
3. Click **"Save"**

**Note:** Fee changes affect new invoices only, not existing ones.

---

### View Admission Invoices

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Admission Invoices"** in the menu
2. View all invoices generated by the Admission Officer
3. Filter by status: Pending, Paid, Overdue
4. Click on an invoice to see the full breakdown and linked payment proofs

---

### Manage Tuition Payments

Tuition payments are separate from admission payment proofs. They cover ongoing semester fees for enrolled students.

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Tuition Payments"** in the menu
2. View all tuition payment records
3. Click on a record to see payment details
4. Manually record cash payments made at the cashier:
   - Select the student
   - Enter amount, payment method, and reference number
   - Upload receipt image (optional)
   - Click **"Record Payment"**

---

### Generate Receipt

**Steps:**
1. After verifying or recording a payment, click **"View Receipt"**
2. Review the receipt details
3. Print or save as PDF for the student

---

### Generate Statement of Account

**Steps:**
1. Search and select a student
2. Click **"Statement"**
3. Select date range
4. View or print the statement showing:
   - All charges
   - All payments
   - Current outstanding balance

---

### Manage Financial Clearances

At end of semester, students must be financially cleared before records are processed.

**Steps:**
1. Click **"Clearances"** in the menu
2. View students with pending accounting clearance
3. Check each student's outstanding balance
4. Mark as **Cleared** (balance settled) or **Not Cleared** (balance outstanding)
5. Add notes if applicable

See [Clearances](features/clearances.md) for full details.

## Payment Verification Checklist

Before verifying a payment, confirm:

- [ ] Receipt image is clear and readable
- [ ] Amount matches the invoice
- [ ] Payment date is reasonable
- [ ] Reference/transaction number is valid
- [ ] Bank records confirm the transaction
- [ ] No duplicate submission

## Void Approval Queue

When a cashier needs to void an OR transaction outside the 30-minute self-void window — or when they're voiding someone else's transaction — the system submits a **Void Request** to you for approval.

**Path:** Accounting sidebar → **Void Requests** (under "Reports & Setup").

You'll see a table with three tabs: **PENDING · APPROVED · REJECTED**.

For each pending request:
1. Review the OR #, student, amount, and the cashier's stated reason.
2. Click **Approve** to apply the void (payment is marked voided; balance reversed on the student's account; voided row stays for audit).
3. Or click **Reject** to leave the payment active. Add notes either way to record your reasoning.

Approved voids are immediately reflected in the student's account and excluded from collection totals on reports.

## Common Questions

**Q: What if the amount paid is less than the invoice?**
A: You can verify partial payments. The remaining balance will show on the student's account.

**Q: Can I reject a payment proof by mistake?**
A: Once rejected, the applicant must upload a new proof. Contact admin if it was an error.

**Q: How do I handle cash payments?**
A: Cash payments made at the cashier should be recorded directly in the system via **Payment of Fees**.

**Q: What if a refund is needed?**
A: Refunds are processed manually. Coordinate with the admin for refund procedures.

**Q: A cashier voided a payment by mistake. Can I undo it?**
A: Voids are permanent records (we don't delete from the audit trail). If the void was wrong, record a fresh payment with a new OR # — note in the remarks that it replaces the voided OR.
