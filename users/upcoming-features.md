## Walk-In Enrollment

**Coming Soon**

Walk-in enrollment allows admission staff to register applicants who visit the school in person, without requiring them to complete the online application first.

### What's New:
- **For Admission Staff:** Quick registration form for walk-in applicants
- **For Registrar:** Direct student creation for special cases
- **Simplified Process:** Fewer required fields for walk-ins
- **Tracking:** System tracks whether applicant applied online or walked in

### Benefits:
- Faster processing for on-site applicants
- No internet required for applicants
- Special handling for transferees and returnees
- Better enrollment tracking and reporting

## Faculty Assignments

**Coming Soon**

Faculty will be able to create and manage assignments directly in the portal.

### What's New:
- **Create Assignments:** Faculty creates assignments per class
- **Student Submissions:** Students submit work through the portal
- **File Attachments:** Support for document uploads (up to 5 files per submission)
- **Deadlines:** Automatic deadline enforcement
- **Grading:** Faculty grades submissions in the system

### For Faculty:
- Create regular assignments or instruction-only posts
- Set deadlines with grace periods
- View and grade all submissions
- Download all submissions at once

### For Students:
- View assignments for enrolled classes
- Submit work before deadline
- Edit submissions until deadline
- View grades and feedback

## Account Restrictions

**Coming Soon**

Students with overdue tuition balances will have limited portal access until payment is made.

### How It Works:
- **Automatic:** System checks for overdue invoices
- **Limited Access:** Restricted students can only access:
  - Messages
  - Payments
  - Profile
- **Full Access Returns:** After balance is paid
- **Manual Override:** Accounting can lift restrictions for special cases

### For Students:
- Clear notification about restriction status
- Easy access to payment section
- Contact information for assistance

### For Accounting:
- View list of restricted students
- Lift restrictions manually when needed
- Track override history
