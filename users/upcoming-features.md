## Account Restrictions

**Coming Soon**

Students with overdue tuition balances will have limited portal access until payment is made.

### How It Works:
- **Automatic:** System checks for overdue invoices
- **Limited Access:** Restricted students can only access:
  - Messages
  - Payments
  - Profile
- **Full Access Returns:** After balance is paid
- **Manual Override:** Accounting can lift restrictions for special cases

### For Students:
- Clear notification about restriction status
- Easy access to payment section
- Contact information for assistance

### For Accounting:
- View list of restricted students
- Lift restrictions manually when needed
- Track override history
