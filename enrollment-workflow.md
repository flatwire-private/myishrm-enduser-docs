# Enrollment Workflow

A complete guide to the MyISHRM enrollment process from application to student registration.

---

## How Enrollment Works

MyISHRM uses a **5-step enrollment workflow** that involves multiple departments working together to ensure proper verification at each stage.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                      ENROLLMENT WORKFLOW                            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                                                     │
│   Step 1: APPLICANT                                                 │
│   ───────────────────                                               │
│   Submits application online with required documents                │
│                              ↓                                      │
│   Step 2: ADMISSION                                                 │
│   ─────────────────                                                 │
│   Reviews application → Approves → Generates invoice                │
│                              ↓                                      │
│   Step 3: APPLICANT                                                 │
│   ───────────────────                                               │
│   Pays admission fees → Uploads payment proof                       │
│                              ↓                                      │
│   Step 4: ACCOUNTING                                                │
│   ──────────────────                                                │
│   Verifies payment proof against bank records                       │
│                              ↓                                      │
│   Step 5: REGISTRAR                                                 │
│   ─────────────────                                                 │
│   Converts applicant to student → Account created                   │
│                                                                     │
│                    ══════════════════════                           │
│                    WELCOME, NEW STUDENT!                            │
│                    ══════════════════════                           │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Application Submission (Applicant)

The enrollment process begins when you submit your application.

**What you need to do:**
1. Go to [https://myishrm.flatwire.io/apply](https://myishrm.flatwire.io/apply)
2. Create an account with your email
3. Complete the application form:
   - Personal information
   - Contact details
   - Educational background
   - Guardian information
   - Program selection
4. Upload required documents:
   - Valid ID (government-issued)
   - Birth Certificate
   - Transcript of Records / Form 137
   - 2x2 ID Photo
5. Submit your application

**What happens next:**
- You receive an application number (e.g., APP-2026-00001)
- Your status becomes **"Pending"**
- Wait for Admission to review your application

**Detailed guide:** [Applicant Guide](users/applicant.md)

---

### Step 2: Application Review (Admission)

The Admission Officer reviews your application.

**What Admission does:**
1. Reviews your personal information
2. Checks all uploaded documents
3. Verifies documents are clear and complete
4. May schedule an interview (if required)
5. Approves your application
6. Generates an invoice for admission fees

**What happens next:**
- Your status becomes **"Approved"**
- An invoice is generated automatically
- You receive notification with payment instructions

**Detailed guide:** [Admission Officer Guide](users/admission-officer.md)

---

### Step 3: Payment (Applicant)

After approval, you need to pay the admission fees.

**What you need to do:**
1. Login to your applicant portal
2. Go to the **"Payment"** section
3. View your invoice showing:
   - Fee breakdown (admission fee, registration fee, etc.)
   - Total amount due
   - Payment deadline
4. Pay through any of these methods:
   - Bank deposit/transfer
   - Cash payment at school
   - Other approved payment methods
5. Upload your payment proof:
   - Take a clear photo/scan of your receipt
   - Enter payment details (amount, date, reference number)
   - Upload the file (JPEG, PNG, or PDF, max 5MB)
6. Submit for verification

**What happens next:**
- Your payment status becomes **"Pending Verification"**
- Wait for Accounting to verify your payment

**Detailed guide:** [Applicant Guide - Payment Section](users/applicant.md#step-6-pay-admission-fees)

---

### Step 4: Payment Verification (Accounting)

The Accounting Staff verifies your payment.

**What Accounting does:**
1. Reviews your uploaded payment proof
2. Verifies against bank records:
   - Amount matches the invoice?
   - Reference number is valid?
   - Payment date is correct?
3. Approves or rejects the payment

**If Approved:**
- Invoice status becomes **"Paid"**
- You're now ready for student conversion

**If Rejected:**
- You'll see the rejection reason in your portal
- Upload a new payment proof with corrections

**Detailed guide:** [Accounting Staff Guide](users/accounting-staff.md)

---

### Step 5: Student Conversion (Registrar)

The Registrar converts you from applicant to student.

**What Registrar does:**
1. Sees that your payment is verified
2. Reviews your application one final time
3. Converts you to a student
4. System creates your student account

**What happens:**
- You receive your **Student ID** (e.g., 2026-00001)
- You receive login credentials for the Student Portal
- Your status becomes **"Enrolled"**

**You are now a student!**

**Detailed guide:** [Registrar Guide](users/registrar.md)

## Track Your Application Status

| Status | What It Means | What To Do |
|--------|---------------|------------|
| **Pending** | Application submitted, awaiting review | Wait for Admission review |
| **Under Review** | Being evaluated by Admission | Wait for decision |
| **For Interview** | Interview scheduled | Attend your interview |
| **Approved** | Accepted! Invoice generated | Pay and upload proof |
| **Payment Pending** | Payment proof uploaded | Wait for verification |
| **Payment Verified** | Payment confirmed by Accounting | Wait for conversion |
| **Enrolled** | You're a student! | Login to Student Portal |
| **Rejected** | Application not accepted | See rejection reason |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is there a separate payment step?**
A: The payment verification step ensures that fees are properly received before enrollment, protecting both you and the school from discrepancies.

**Q: Can Admission convert me directly to a student?**
A: No. The workflow requires payment verification by Accounting and final conversion by Registrar to ensure proper checks at each stage.

**Q: What if I paid but didn't upload proof?**
A: You must upload your payment proof in the portal. Accounting cannot verify your payment without seeing the receipt.

**Q: How long does the whole process take?**
A: Typically 1-2 weeks from application submission to becoming a student, assuming timely payment and complete documents.

**Q: What if my payment is rejected?**
A: Read the rejection reason provided, correct the issue (e.g., upload a clearer receipt), and submit a new payment proof.

**Q: Can I check my application status anytime?**
A: Yes! Login to your applicant portal to see your current status and timeline of all status changes.
